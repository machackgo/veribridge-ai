§ 01

Executive Platform Overview

Three-sided Marketplace

A multi-sided platform with one shared verified profile graph at the center. Students build proof-backed profiles; recruiters discover and invite verified candidates; universities receive privacy-respecting aggregate analytics on cohort readiness and skill trends.

The Three-Sided Platform

Sides · Value Exchanged · Trust Anchor
Side A — Primary User

Students

Verified .edu identity. Build a proof-of-skill profile, generate tailored applications, track outcomes, and receive recruiter invitations.

B2C · FREE TIER
Side B — B2B Customer

Recruiters

Search verified students by role-readiness, skill evidence and visa compatibility. Send invitations, track conversion, manage company brand.

B2B · SUBSCRIPTION
Side C — Institutional B2B

Universities

Aggregate, anonymized analytics for career centers — readiness by major, skill gaps, employer engagement, recruiter trust scores.

B2B · INSTITUTIONAL
PILLAR 01
Verified .edu identity
Email-domain verification + university-issued artifacts; the trust anchor that powers proof-of-skill and recruiter signal quality.
PILLAR 02
Proof over claims
Every skill carries evidence — GitHub commits, coursework, portfolio links, certificates — surfaced as verifiable proof cards.
PILLAR 03
Outcome learning loop
Edits, applications and interview outcomes feed back into the recommendation, scoring and skill-gap engines.
PILLAR 04
Privacy-first network effects
Universities and benchmarks operate on anonymized cohorts; students retain explicit visibility and consent controls.
