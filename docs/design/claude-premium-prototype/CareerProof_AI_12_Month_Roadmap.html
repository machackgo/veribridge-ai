§ 01

Executive Summary

Three-sided Marketplace · 12-Month Plan

CareerProof AI is a verified .edu career-readiness platform built on proof-of-skill. Students prove skills with evidence; recruiters discover verified candidates; universities receive privacy-respecting cohort analytics. This document is the production plan for shipping all three sides in 12 months.

The platform — three sides, one verified profile graph

Sides · Value · Trust Anchor
Side A — Primary User

Students

Verified .edu identity. Build a proof-backed profile, generate tailored applications, track outcomes, prep for interviews, opt into recruiter discovery.

B2C · FREE TIER
Side B — B2B Customer

Recruiters

Search verified students by readiness, evidence, visa-compatibility. Send invitations, manage company brand, track conversion. Subscription billing.

B2B · SUBSCRIPTION
Side C — Institutional B2B

Universities

Aggregate, anonymized analytics for career centers — readiness by major, skill gaps, recruiter trust scores, employer engagement, outcome reports.

B2B · INSTITUTIONAL
Differentiator — proof over claims. Every skill on a CareerProof profile carries evidence: GitHub commits, coursework, portfolio links, certifications, deployed projects. This is what powers recruiter trust, university benchmarking, and the AI scoring that students get back.
