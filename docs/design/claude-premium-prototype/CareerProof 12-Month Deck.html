02Problem & Opportunity
Three Trust Gaps

Three trust gaps. One verified profile graph.

Students, recruiters, and universities all want the same outcome — better-matched early-career hires — but each side is missing something the other two have. We close the loop.

Student gap

Career readiness

Students don't know which roles they're ready for, what skills are missing, or how to prove what they actually know. Resumes flatten years of work into a page of bullets.

Self-reported readiness ≪ employer reality
Recruiter gap

Trust in early-career talent

Recruiters can't verify resume claims at scale. GitHub, coursework, and portfolios sit in 5 different places. Visa-compatibility filtering is broken or manual.

Time-to-verify per candidate 10–30 min
University gap

Cohort-level analytics

Career centers run on surveys. They can't see which majors are job-ready, where skill gaps are widening, or which employers are converting — privately and at the cohort level.

Outcome data lag 6–18 months
Opportunity
A verified .edu profile graph anchored on proof-of-skill — not claims — that all three sides depend on. The same data improves student outcomes, recruiter conversion, and university benchmarking.

09Security, Privacy & Compliance
RBAC · Consent · FERPA-aware

Privacy is the architecture, not a setting.

Raw student data never leaves the consent envelope. Universities only see aggregate cubes computed against k-anon thresholds; recruiters only see profiles where the student has opted in.

SC-01

.edu & Recruiter Verification

Domain-bound student identity · company-bound recruiter identity · artifact verification.
Identity
SC-02

Role-Based Access Control

Four primary roles — student, recruiter, university, internal admin — enforced at API + DB layers.
RBAC
SC-03

Consent Manager

Per-scope visibility · explicit recruiter discovery opt-in · revocation flows · UI mirrors policy.
Consent
SC-04

PII Vault & Encryption

Field-level encryption · KMS-managed keys · separation of audit and operational reads.
Encryption
SC-05

Anonymization

k-anonymity thresholds · cohort floors · suppression rules · quarterly anonymization tests.
k-Anon
SC-06

Audit Log

Append-only · WORM-style ledger · queryable · who-what-when on every read of student data.
Audit
SC-07

Retention & Right-to-Erase

TTL on every data class · student-driven erasure · cascading deletion across vector store.
Retention
SC-08

FERPA-Aware Posture

Institutional DPA template · controls aligned to FERPA · default-private visa disclosure.
FERPA
Privacy boundary
Three-sided does not mean three copies of the data. Aggregates, recruiter views, and student profiles are computed views over the same consent-gated source — with audit trails on every traversal.
