ℹ Chrome Screen Recording
This tab records a WebM video using MediaRecorder + getDisplayMedia (OS-level). Select Entire Screen in the picker to capture hardware-decoded fullscreen video — standard tab capture cannot do this.

Recording is stored at ~500 kbps. After stopping, the video is uploaded to VeriBridge where keyframes are extracted for evidence. Max recording: 5 minutes / 100 MB.
🔒 Frames are captured at low quality (JPEG 50%) and sent only to your VeriBridge backend. Sensitive fields are masked by the extension before upload. Local file paths and auth tokens are automatically redacted.
